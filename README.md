# RELAPSED

> *A branching narrative visual novel where the fate of your callers rests in your hands.*

## ⚠️ Content Warning / Disclaimer
**RELAPSED deals with highly sensitive themes including suicide, domestic abuse, addiction, abandonment, and loss.** We take these issues very seriously. If you identify with any aspect of our game or are currently going through a difficult time, please reach out for professional help. It is never too late to do so.

---

## 📖 Synopsis

In *RELAPSED*, you step into the shoes of Alejandro Morales, a deeply depressed man who has lost his passion for life. In an attempt to move forward, Alejandro takes a job at a psychological support call center. 

Every day, you answer phone calls from individuals in extremely fragile and vulnerable situations. Through your own decisions, you must navigate these conversations, offer the best support possible, and carefully manage the psychological state of your callers. Your words have weight, and your choices will directly determine the outcome of their lives.

## 💻 Technical Implementation

This project was developed collaboratively, focusing on weaving complex narrative design with robust logic using the **Fungus framework** within **Unity**.

* **Branching Narrative Architecture:** Engineered complex, multi-layered dialogue trees using Fungus flowcharts, ensuring smooth transitions between different conversational states.
* **Impactful Player Choices:** Implemented global variables and state-tracking systems to monitor player decisions across different calls, triggering distinct narrative branches and multiple endings.
* **Collaborative Workflow:** Integrated writing, audio, and visual assets into the logic flow in a collaborative team environment, maintaining clean state management throughout the game's scenes.
* **Audio-Visual Pacing:** Programmed custom delays, text-typing speeds, and audio cues within the dialogue system to emphasize the psychological weight of the conversations.

## ⚙️ Installation & Running

1. Download the latest release from the [Itch.io](https://tokpary.itch.io/relapsed).
2. Extract the `.zip` file into a local folder.
3. Run `RELAPSED.exe`.
4. *Recommendation: Play in a quiet environment with headphones to fully immerse yourself in the narrative.*
